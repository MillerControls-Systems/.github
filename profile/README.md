# Miller Controls & Systems

**Industrial data collection and PLC connectivity.**

Miller Controls & Systems focuses on connecting industrial control systems with the software that makes their data useful. Our goal is to help engineers bring equipment data into monitoring, analysis, and operational workflows.

## OpenSy

**OpenSy** is our industrial data collection and programmable logic controller (PLC) connectivity project. Its focus is making data from industrial equipment more accessible to the applications and people that need it.

Our priorities for OpenSy are:

- **PLC connectivity** — connecting industrial controllers with data collection software.
- **Data collection** — gathering equipment and process data for downstream use.
- **Practical integration** — making collected data useful in monitoring and analysis workflows.
- **Maintainability** — emphasizing clear configuration, documentation, and understandable software.
